Mind the Grounding Gap

Recorded responses of eight voice agents to a spoken question, each with access to a search tool.

Voice agents can answer a spoken question without consulting the search tool they were given. This page plays the recorded responses of four commercial systems and four open-weight systems: MoshiRAG, and Nemotron before and after fine-tuning (SFT, then SFT + GRPO). Every response is annotated with three marks: whether the model searched, whether a retrieved passage contained the reference answer, and whether the spoken answer was correct.

This page shows one question from the 132-question evaluation set, with the recorded response of every system. All 132 questions are scored in the paper.

Reading the marks

  • Searched — the model called the search tool at least once.
  • Retrieved — a retrieved passage contains the expected answer.
  • Correct — the spoken answer matches the expected answer. Open Details on any response to read its full transcript and searches.

About the audio

Waiting time is cut: leading and trailing silence is trimmed and pauses longer than 1.5 s are shortened to under 0.5 s. Speech, repetitions and mistakes are left untouched, so playback length is not response latency.
